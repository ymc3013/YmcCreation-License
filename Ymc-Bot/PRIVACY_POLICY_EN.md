PRIVACY POLICY — YMC-BOT 

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction 

Ymc-Bot is a Discord bot developed and managed as part of the YmcCreation project.

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected, used, stored, and protected when using Ymc-Bot.

The person responsible for the bot is the director of YmcCreation, known by the Discord username ymc3013.

2. Data That May Be Processed 

Depending on the features used and the server configuration, Ymc-Bot may process the following information:

Discord user, server, channel, and role IDs. Discord usernames and publicly available profile information required for the bot's functionality. Moderation information, including warnings, kicks, and bans. Information related to member verification and applications to join YmcCreation. Information required to manage support tickets. Project-related information stored or synchronized with services used by YmcCreation. 

The actual data processed depends on the features enabled. Ymc-Bot should not collect information unrelated to its intended functionality.

3. How Data Is Used 

Data may be used to:

Provide the bot's features. Manage server moderation and security. Verify server members. Handle support tickets and applications. Manage YmcCreation project information. Diagnose errors and improve the bot's reliability. 

4. Data Retention 

Some data may be stored temporarily or for longer periods, depending on its purpose and the bot's configuration.

Data should not be retained longer than necessary for its intended purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Users may contact the person responsible for the bot to ask about their data and, where applicable, request correction or deletion.

5. Data Sharing 

Ymc-Bot must not sell users' personal data.

Certain information may be accessible to authorized staff members for moderation, support, or YmcCreation management.

Data may also be processed by Discord or third-party services required for the bot's operation, such as hosting providers or project management services.

These services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Security 

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or accidental disclosure.

However, no computer system can guarantee absolute security.

Bot tokens, passwords, and other confidential credentials must never be shared publicly.

7. User Rights 

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR where applicable, users may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of, or object to the processing of their personal data.

To exercise these rights or ask questions about privacy, users may contact the person responsible for Ymc-Bot through an official YmcCreation contact method.

A request may require reasonable information to verify the requester's identity.

8. Minors 

Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and the laws applicable in their country.

Personal data belonging to minors must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy 

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to Ymc-Bot, its features, or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version should be published through an official YmcCreation channel.
